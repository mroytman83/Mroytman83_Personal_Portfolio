About Me

I am a Data Science Engineer with a passion for building scalable, data-driven applications that automate processes, enhance decision-making, and make complex data more accessible and actionable. I thrive at the intersection of data engineering, AI, and automation, where I design robust ETL/ELT pipelines, architect data streams, develop information retrieval systems, and leverage machine learning and NLP to extract insights from unstructured data.



I graduated from the University of Wisconsin, Madison, with a degree in Data Science and Economics. My coursework covered machine learning, big data systems, object-oriented programming, mathematics, statistics, and econometrics, providing me with a strong technical foundation in data science and analytics.

Beyond academics, I was actively involved in entrepreneurial and research-driven initiatives, including:

  • Writing a publication on Congressional Insider Trading.
  • Co-founding Cardinal Trading Group, an algorithmic trading club focused on quantitative finance and machine learning.
  • Competing in CS Nest and Transcend Pitch Competitions, presenting AI-driven solutions.
  • Delivering a talk at ML+X, a community dedicated to machine learning applications.
  • Participating in a material science informatics research group Informatics Skunkworks


I have worked on a range of technical projects and real-world applications, including developing full-stack AI agent applications for natural language to SQL retrieval, automating infrastructure maintenance to streamline dependency and security updates, and implementing user tracking systems to provide data-driven insights for R&D. Additionally, I have built scheduled web scrapers for financial sentiment analysis and applied computer vision techniques to sports analytics.



My personal mission is to leverage data, AI, and automation to solve real-world problems and create scalable, high-impact solutions that drive meaningful change. I am particularly interested in applying these technologies in manufacturing, fintech, proptech, and energy, where data-driven innovation can optimize processes, improve efficiency, and enhance decision-making.



In addition to my work in data science and AI, I have a deep interest in sports and fitness. I am an avid weightlifter, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu practitioner, MMA enthusiast, and rock climber. I strongly believe in the dynamic balance between mind and body, which is why one of my favorite quotes comes from Socrates:

"No man has the right to be an amateur in the matter of physical training; it is a shame for a man to grow old without seeing the beauty and strength of which his body is capable."

To me, mental and physical discipline go hand in hand, and both are essential for growth and success.



I am always open to new opportunities, collaborations, and discussions! Whether you're interested in working together on data-driven projects, AI applications, or just want to talk tech and fitness, feel free to reach out via the contact tab!
